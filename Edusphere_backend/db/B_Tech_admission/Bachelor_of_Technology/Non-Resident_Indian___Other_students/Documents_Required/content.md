For Resident Indian Students
  * Allotment Letter issued by K J Somaiya School of Engineering for admission to First Year B. Tech. in Academic year 2025-26.
  * Scorecard for at least one of the following: Scholastic Assessment Test (SAT) 2024/SAT 2025, JEE-Main (Session 1 and 2) 2025, Common University Entrance Test (CUET) 2025, MHT-CET 2025, and PERA-CET 2025
  * Mark list for HSC or Equivalent examination (Self attested copy).
  * Mark list for S.S.C. or Equivalent examination (Self attested copy).
  * Leaving Certificate from the last attended institution (Self attested copy).
  * Caste Certificate, Caste Validity, if candidate belongs to reserve category under SC/ST/OBC /VJ-NT (Self attested copy).
  * Aadhaar Card, Two passport size Photographs of the candidate.
  * Maharashtra State Domicile Certificate.
  * Indian Nationality Certificate (If not mentioned on the Leaving Certificate).
  * Gap Certificate/ Affidavit (if HSC passed before/prior to Academic Year 2024-25).
  * Equivalence Certificate / Migration Certificate of Concern Board (if student passed HSC from other than Maharashtra Board).
  * Medical fitness certificate also indicating the blood group issued by registered medical practitioner.

For Non-Resident Indian & Other students 
  * Allotment Letter issued by K J Somaiya School of Engineering for admission to First Year B. Tech. in Academic year 2025-26.
  * Scorecard for at least one of the following: Scholastic Assessment Test (SAT) 2024/ SAT 2025, JEE-Main (Session 1 and 2) 2025, Common University Entrance Test (CUET) 2025, MHT-CET 2025, and PERA-CET 2025
  * Mark list for HSC or Equivalent examination (Self attested copy).
  * Mark list for S.S.C. or Equivalent examination (Self attested copy)
  * Eligibility certificate from Association of Universities (AIU) New Delhi (if applicable)
  * Leaving Certificate from the last attended institution (Self attested copy)
  * Aadhaar Card, (if available and Self attested),Two passport size Photographs of the candidate
  * Proof of NRI 
    * A letter issued by an Indian embassy indicating NRI status of parent/ sponsor/ candidate
    * Certificate issued by the Indian Embassy indicating NRI status of Sponsor/ parent/ candidate
    * Off Shore Certificate issued by the organization (In case of ward of Merchant Navy)
  * Passport’s copy of parent/sponsor/ candidate
  * PIO (OCI) card copy (In case if the candidate is admitted under PIO(OCI) category).
  * Gap Certificate/ Affidavit (if HSC passed before prior to Academic Year 2024-25).
  * Medical fitness certificate also indicating the blood group issued by registered medical practitioner.
