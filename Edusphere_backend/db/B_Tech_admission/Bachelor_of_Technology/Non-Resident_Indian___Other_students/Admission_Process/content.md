Step
1
Candidates (pursuing HSC 2025 /completed HSC in 2024 or before) willing to pursue B. Tech degree can submit the online preadmission form available on college [Admission website](https://application.somaiya.edu/widget/#/signup?groupId=1) with payment of non-refundable/ non-transferable application fees Rs. 2000/-
Step
2
Admission process will be carried out in different rounds based on different entrance examinations: SAT 2024/2025/ JEE Main Session 2025 /PERA-CET 2025/ MHT-CET 2025/CUET 2025. Visit important dates section on college [website](https://kjsce.somaiya.edu/en/admission/btech?vthmstablink=vtmhs2) for schedule of rounds.
Step
3
Candidates can apply any one or more than one rounds within the same pre-application based entrance examinations using the login credentials (registered email and password).
Step
4
A provisional seat offered or not offered (based on the merit, programme preference and availability of seats taken together) will be communicated via registered email.
