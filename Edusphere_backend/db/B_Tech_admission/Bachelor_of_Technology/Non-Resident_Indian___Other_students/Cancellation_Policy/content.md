Cancellation policy for First Year B.Tech. 2025-26 
  * This cancellation policy will be effective from August 15, 2025.
  * Admitted student can apply for refund at Somaiya myaccount (myaccount.somaiya.edu) Portal.
  * UGC Notification issued in October 2018 on Refund of Fees clause 4.1.3 and clause 4.1.4 shall apply (clause 4.1.3 is reproduced below for ready reference)


Sr. No. | Percent of Refund of Fees | Point of time when refund is applied  
---|---|---  
1 | 100%* | 15 days or more before the formally notified last date# of admission  
2 | 90% | Less than 15 days before formally notified last date# of admission  
3 | 80% | 15 days or less after formally notified last date# of admission  
4 | 50% | 30 days or less, but more than 15 days after formally notified last date# of admission  
5 | 00% | More than 30 days after formally notified last date# of admission  
*Less processing fee as per UGC Refund Policy Guideline 2018 clause 4.1.4: In case of (1) in the table above, the HEI concerned shall deduct an amount not more than 5% of the fees paid by the student, subject to a maximum of Rs. 5,000/- as processing charges from the refundable amount.
